Purpose of the Project

The purpose of Druk SkillBridge is to design and develop a web-based freelance service marketplace that provides a structured, transparent, and trustworthy platform for connecting clients with skilled freelancers in Bhutan. The platform addresses the limitations of informal service discovery methods, such as social media and word-of-mouth, by enabling freelancers to create professional profiles, showcase their services, and build credibility through ratings and reviews. At the same time, it allows clients to easily search for, compare, and engage suitable service providers. Additionally, the system promotes accountability through complaint management and administrative oversight, contributing to a more accessible, reliable, and professional freelance services ecosystem in Bhutan.
